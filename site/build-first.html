State and review legend
  • QUEUED: proposed work has not started; BUILDING: work is underway. These are program tracking states and do not claim GitHub acceptance.
  • Needs public spec: no matching ID in any tracked public status manifest.
  • Owner unresolved: a provisional assignment needs partition or owner review, or the proposed owner's repository has no matching spec. Candidate links do not establish authority. An explicit reviewed owner decision is required to promote a candidate to owner spec.

  • Partition review: ownership across repositories still needs a contract decision. Multiple spec links alone do not imply conflicting ownership.
  • Owner review: the proposed owner is unresolved. `OWNER_REVIEW` does not designate a repository.
