Version 1 manifests keep working. | `tests/test_requirement_progress.py::test_v2_verified_and_landed_count_as_delivered`, `::test_v2_sha_off_main_or_missing_demotes_with_a_reason`, `::test_v2_verified_needs_tests_and_unknown_states_are_rejected`, `::test_v2_retired_is_closed_and_leaves_the_denominator`; `tests/test_public_spec_report.py::test_v2_committed_specs_counts_verified_and_demotes_off_main` |
| `ORG-FRONT-R005` | **The status page shows the state breakdown.** The generated page must show a SPECIFIED, LANDED, VERIFIED and RETIRED breakdown with percent delivered for each repository and each spec, and its legend must explain the version 2 states and the demotion rule. | `tests/test_public_spec_report.py::test_v2_committed_specs_counts_verified_and_demotes_off_main` (rendered page) |
